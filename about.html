Who we are

About SignPlay

Many people want to communicate with the Deaf community, but find few ways to start. SignPlay turns that first step into something enjoyable and rewarding.

Learners work through short, focused stages: watch a demo, practise with the camera, then prove it in a quiz or a game. Stars, XP and badges keep progress visible and motivation high, and every account saves its progress so learners can continue anywhere.

SignPlay runs in the browser. There is nothing to install, and the sign checker runs on the learner's own device.

Learn Mode

30 stages in 3 levels, with demos, practice, quizzes and a boss stage at the end of each level.

Game Mode

A mystery game where signs are the clues. Learners practise what they know while solving the case.

Instant camera feedback

Your webcam checks the sign you make, so you get feedback even when you practise alone.
