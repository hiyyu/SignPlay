## 🌟 Executive Overview & Pitch

**Gestigo** is a detective-themed, gamified American Sign Language (ASL) educational platform designed to turn sign language acquisition into an addictive mystery adventure. Instead of passive flashcards, players take on the identity of **Inspector Gestigo** — an ASL cyber-forensics investigator solving high-stakes heists across 30 progressive stages.

### 🎮 Dual-Core Experience (Requested Architecture)
1. **Academy (Learn Mode)**:
   - A structured curriculum spanning **3 Levels** (Beginner Foundations, Intermediate Everyday Life, Advanced Conversational Fluency).
   - **10 Stages per Level (Total 30 Stages)** with full biomechanics, palm orientation guidance, memory tricks, and real-time practice.
2. **Mystery Mode (Play Mode)**:
   - A narrative detective quest: "The Case of the Putrajaya Dragon Seal Heist".
   - Interrogate witnesses communicating in ASL, crack laser vault ciphers, track escape vehicles, and face off against masterminds in timed Boss Encounters!

## 📚 30-Stage Comprehensive Curriculum

### Level 1: Foundations (Stages 1–10)
- **Stage 1**: The Manual Alphabet (A–Z) — Static handshapes and fingerspelling clarity.
- **Stage 2**: Numbers & Counting (1–100) — Digits, palm inward/outward rules.
- **Stage 3**: Time & Calendar — Clock times, weekdays, seasons.
- **Stage 4**: Core Survival & Greetings — Hello, please, thank you, sorry, help.
- **Stage 5**: Personal Pronouns & Identity — Me, you, name, detective role.
- **Stage 6**: Family & Relationships — Gender zones (forehead vs. chin), mom, dad, brother.
- **Stage 7**: Colors, Shapes, & Clothes — Descriptors and wardrobe items.
- **Stage 8**: Food & Drink Essentials — Water, coffee, eat, meal signs.
- **Stage 9**: Home & Daily Routine — Room layouts, doors, safe coordinates.
- **Stage 10 (Boss Stage)**: Survival Gauntlet — Undercover speakeasy rapid-fire interrogation.

### Level 2: Everyday Life & Interactions (Stages 11–20)
- **Stage 11**: Emotions & Feelings — Facial affect, happy, sad, angry, tired.
- **Stage 12**: Health & The Body — Directional pain, doctor, hospital triage.
- **Stage 13**: Restaurant & Dining Out — Bistro rendezvous, ordering, check.
- **Stage 14**: Shopping & Money — Prices, cash, expensive, pawn shop appraisal.
- **Stage 15**: Hobbies & Sports — Music conducting, reading, eSports disguise.
- **Stage 16**: School & Education — Person marker (Teacher/Student), library archive.
- **Stage 17**: Work & Professions — Corporate office, mainframe computer, boss.
- **Stage 18**: Weather & Nature — Rain, storm, cold, sea wall escape.
- **Stage 19**: Places Around Town — Store, bank, transit coordinates.
- **Stage 20 (Boss Stage)**: Community Sting — Bazaar coordinated multi-squad tactical operation.

### Level 3: Conversational Fluency & Culture (Stages 21–30)
- **Stage 21**: Direction & Spatial Verbs — Subject-object vector trajectory (I help you / you help me).
- **Stage 22**: Questions & Non-Manual Markers — Wh- questions (furrowed brows) vs. Yes/No.
- **Stage 23**: Travel & Transportation — Classifiers, airport tarmac intercept.
- **Stage 24**: Emergencies & Safety — High-intensity emergency signs, arson evacuation.
- **Stage 25**: Technology & Modern Life — Smartphone, internet web, cyber bunker.
- **Stage 26**: Classifiers & Visual Scaling — CL:3 (vehicle), CL:1 (person), CL:B (barrier).
- **Stage 27**: Storytelling & Narratives — Role shifting, transitions (HAPPEN, FINISH, TRUE).
- **Stage 28**: Idioms, Slang, & Expressive Nuances — Cultural idioms (PAH!, TRAIN-GONE).
- **Stage 29**: Deaf Culture & Etiquette — Respect, community norms, eye-contact rules.
- **Stage 30 (Boss Stage)**: The Fluent Masterclass — High-stakes rooftop observatory live showdown against the Mastermind!

## ⚡ How to Run

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch (Zero Install)
Simply open `index.html` in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.

### Option 2: Python Local Server
```bash
python serve.py
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8000`
